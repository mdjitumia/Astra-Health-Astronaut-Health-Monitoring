# 🚀 ASTRAHEALTH

### Autonomous Astronaut Health Monitoring & Decision Support System

> **Protect the Astronaut.**

ASTRAHEALTH is an AI-assisted health intelligence concept for
long-duration human space missions. Instead of looking at isolated
health measurements, it combines physiological and behavioral signals,
builds a personalized baseline, detects meaningful changes over time,
explains potential risk contributors, and supports the astronaut with
actionable next-step guidance.

**Core idea:**\
**Don't just monitor health. Understand the change, explain the risk,
and support the next action.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🛰️ NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 challenge:

**Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions**

Long-duration missions expose astronauts to radiation, isolation and
confinement, altered gravity, and hostile environments. ASTRAHEALTH
addresses this challenge through personalized monitoring, longitudinal
trend analysis, explainable AI, early warning, and decision support.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 The Problem

Important health changes during a long-duration mission may develop
gradually.

A slightly higher heart rate, reduced sleep, lower HRV, or increased
stress may not be significant individually. But when several signals
deviate from an astronaut's normal pattern and persist over time, their
combined trend may deserve attention.

> **"Is this change unusual for this astronaut?"**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 💡 Our Solution

ASTRAHEALTH creates a personalized health-intelligence layer:

**Collect → Compare → Detect → Explain → Act → Re-monitor**

1.  Collect health and behavioral indicators
2.  Establish an individual baseline
3.  Compare new measurements with that baseline
4.  Detect abnormal trends and patterns
5.  Estimate potential health risks
6.  Explain the main contributing factors
7.  Provide decision-support recommendations
8.  Re-monitor after intervention

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ⭐ Key Capabilities

-   ❤️ **Multi-parameter health monitoring** --- heart rate, HRV, SpO₂,
    respiration, sleep, stress, activity and recovery indicators
-   🧬 **Personalized baseline** --- evaluates deviation from an
    individual's normal pattern
-   📈 **Longitudinal trend detection** --- identifies persistent
    changes instead of relying only on single readings
-   🤖 **AI/ML risk analysis** --- supports multi-signal risk estimation
-   🔍 **Explainable AI** --- shows which factors contributed to a
    detected risk
-   🚨 **Early warning** --- Normal → Deviation → Persistent Trend →
    Risk → Early Warning
-   🧠 **AI Health Copilot concept** --- natural-language explanations
    of system-generated insights
-   🏃 **Countermeasure decision support** --- recovery, workload,
    exercise/sleep review, increased monitoring, and escalation when
    appropriate
-   🌌 **Deep-space operation concept** --- local analysis first, with
    priority information prepared for mission control
-   ⚡ **Adaptive monitoring** --- increases attention when the
    astronaut's condition changes

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🧠 Intelligence Architecture

``` text
Health Data
    ↓
Data Preprocessing
    ↓
Feature Engineering
    ↓
Personal Baseline Modeling
    ↓
Anomaly / Trend Detection
    ↓
Risk Prediction
    ↓
Explainable AI
    ↓
Safety / Rule Layer
    ↓
Decision Support
    ↓
Astronaut / Mission Control
    ↓
Re-monitor
```

The proposed architecture separates **risk estimation, explanation, and
decision support**. Generative AI is intended to explain
system-generated insights rather than independently determine medical
risk.

## 🖥️ Current Prototype

The public prototype demonstrates the core user experience through an
interactive astronaut health dashboard.

### Current prototype stack

-   **HTML5**
-   **CSS3**
-   **JavaScript**
-   Interactive health visualization
-   Simulated astronaut health data
-   Personalized baseline comparison
-   Risk visualization
-   Explainability interface
-   AI health-copilot concept
-   Mission timeline and crew overview
-   Deep-space and adaptive-monitoring concepts

### Data status

The current prototype uses **NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)**
— open-access spaceflight/space-relevant physiological and behavioral data. 
prototype currently combines NASA-referenced data structures with simulated astronaut health data.

Future versions are intended to integrate validated biomedical and
spaceflight datasets, wearable streams, and real-time physiological
data.

## ⚠️ Prototype & Safety Disclaimer

ASTRAHEALTH is a **research and software prototype** created for the
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

It is **not a certified medical device and does not provide clinical
diagnosis**. Current health-risk outputs, alerts, explanations, and
recommendations are experimental demonstrations and require validation
with appropriate biomedical/spaceflight data and qualified domain
experts before any real-world medical use.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🌍 Vision

As humans travel farther from Earth, immediate access to Earth-based
support becomes more difficult. ASTRAHEALTH explores how intelligent
onboard software could help astronauts understand changing health
signals and make better-informed decisions locally.
